## 2. Gramer Konuları (ÖNCELİK — Unit 6 sonrası)

### 2.1 — 7A · Past Modals (Geçmişe Yönelik Modallar)
*Kaynak: IMG_9430 (Grammar Bank 7A, p.144), IMG_9450 (Workbook p.118), IMG_9443 (cloze)*

**Çıkarım (deduction) — `modal + have + V3`:**
- **must have + V3** → eminim oldu/öyleydi. *"She must have worked really hard."*
- **might / may / could have + V3** → belki oldu/olmadı (ihtimal). *"There might have been an accident."*
- **can't have / couldn't have + V3** → kesinlikle olmadı / imkânsız. *"You can't have finished all of it."* (must have'in olumsuzu = **can't have**, *mustn't have DEĞİL*)

**Eleştiri / pişmanlık (advice/regret) — `should/ought to + have + V3`:**
- **should have + V3** → yapmalıydı (ama yapmadı). *"We should have turned left."*
- **shouldn't have + V3** → yapmamalıydı (ama yaptı). *"You shouldn't have put in so much chilli."*
- **ought to have + V3** = should have alternatifi. *"I ought to have told you earlier."*
- Karşılaştırma: *She **should have** phoned me* (söyledim ama aramadı) ≠ *She **must have** phoned me* (eminim aradı).

**Pratik (IMG_9450a cevaplı):** might have been (accident) · might have broken · must have left · can't have put · can't have drunk · must have worked · can't have finished · might have got · must have switched.
**Pratik (IMG_9450b cevaplı):** should have taken · shouldn't have put · should have kept · should have come · should have left · should have told · shouldn't have bought.

### 2.2 — 8A · The Passive (Tüm Formlar) + Have/Get Something Done + Impersonal Passive
*Kaynak: IMG_9426 (tahta), IMG_9432 (Grammar Bank 8A p.146), IMG_9453 (Workbook p.122), IMG_9458 (SB p.78), IMG_9431 (Teacher's Guide)*

**Passive temel:** `be + V3 (past participle)`. Zaman, **be** ile değişir:
| Tense | Passive |
|-------|---------|
| Present simple | am/is/are + V3 |
| Past simple | was/were + V3 |
| Present perfect | have/has been + V3 |
| Past perfect | had been + V3 |
| Present continuous | is/are being + V3 |
| Future / modal | will be / can be + V3 |

- Fail (agent) belirtmek için **by**: *"Pizza was invented **by** the Italians."*
- Kullanım: eylem önemli, faili önemsiz/bilinmiyorsa.

**Have / Get something done (causative):** `have/get + object + V3`
- Başkasına yaptırma: *"I had my hair cut." / "We had a burglar alarm installed."*
- Başımıza gelen (kötü) olay: *"I had my iPhone stolen." / "We had our car broken into."*

**Impersonal / reporting passive (haber dili):** know, say, believe, think, report, expect, understand fiilleriyle.
- **It is said/thought/believed that + cümle:** *"It is thought that the murderer is a woman."*
- **S + is said/thought to + inf / to have + V3:** *"The murderer is thought to be a woman." / "He is believed to have left the country."*

**Pratik (IMG_9432a → passive):** has been stolen · is being painted · will be held · hadn't been found · ...
**Pratik (IMG_9453a → impersonal passive):** is believed to · is thought that · is said to · are understood to · is expected that · are thought to.
**Pratik (IMG_9458b → active/passive seç):** being caught · landed · was spotted · snatched · was demonstrating · was caught · had been broken · was called · has been charged.

### 2.3 — 8B · Reporting Verbs (Bildirme Fiilleri)
*Kaynak: IMG_9431/9433 (Grammar Bank 8B p.147/177), IMG_9454 (Workbook), IMG_9459 (SB p.81)*

3 dilbilgisel kalıp:
1. **verb + to-infinitive:** agree, offer, refuse, promise, threaten, decide. *"Jack offered to drive me."*
2. **verb + object + to-infinitive:** advise, persuade, ask, remind, convince, warn, invite. *"Doctors advise us to do more exercise."*
3. **verb + -ing (gerund):** apologize (to sb) for, insist on, accuse sb of, recommend, admit, regret, blame sb for, suggest, deny. *"He admitted stealing the money." / "I apologized for being late."*

- Olumsuz: *not to do* (inf) / *not doing* (gerund). *"He reminded me not to be late."*
- **that-clause** alabilenler: agree, admit, deny, promise, regret. *"Leo admitted that he had stolen the watch."*
- **Pronunciation:** 2 heceli bildirme fiillerinin çoğu 2. hecede vurgulu; **offer, order, threaten** + (admit/promise) 1. hecede. CVC + 2. hece vurgusu → -ed'den önce ünsüz çiftlenir (*regret→regretted, admit→admitted*); 1. hece vurgusu → çiftlenmez (*offer→offered, threaten→threatened*).

**Pratik (IMG_9459c → past tense):** refused · agreed · promised · reminded · advised · invited · denied · admitted · regretted · suggested · accused.

### 2.4 — 9A · Clauses of Contrast & Purpose (Zıtlık & Amaç Cümlecikleri)
*Kaynak: IMG_9434 (Grammar Bank 9A p.148), IMG_9455 (Workbook), tahta WhatsApp 11.55.54 & 13.01.53*

**Contrast (zıtlık):**
- **although / though / even though + cümle:** *"Although the advert said it would last, my dishwasher broke down."* (though daha informal; even though daha güçlü/şaşırtıcı zıtlık)
- **though** cümle sonunda zarf = however. *"I hardly use it, though."*
- **in spite of / despite + noun / V-ing / the fact that + cümle:** *"In spite of the high price..." / "Despite the fact that she's 83..."* (**despite OF yanlış**)

**Purpose (amaç):**
- **to / in order to / so as to + V1:** *"I went to the bank to / in order to / so as to ask for a loan."* (in order to / so as to daha formal)
- **for + noun:** *"I went to the bank for a meeting."* / **for + V-ing** bir şeyin amacı: *"This bag is for carrying my laptop."*
- **so that + subject + modal (can/could/would):** özne değişince kullanılır. *"I took an umbrella so that I wouldn't get wet."*
- Olumsuz amaç: **so as not to / in order not to + V1** (*to not forget DEĞİL*).

**Pratik (IMG_9434a tek kelime, cevaplı):** despite · even · to · as · order · spite · that · although · for · though.

### 2.5 — Gerund & Infinitive / Verb Patterns (6B + yoğun tekrar)
*Kaynak: IMG_9429 (Grammar Bank 6B), IMG_9449 (Workbook p.111), tahta WhatsApp 11.10.02 & 11.41.42, IMG_9424/9425*

- **Gerund (+V-ing):** enjoy, can't stand, avoid, feel like, consider, practise, fancy, admit, couldn't help, don't mind, miss, finish, carry on, keep, suggest, recommend, love/like/hate.
- **To-infinitive (+to V1):** want, would like, decide, plan, hope, need, manage, promise, afford, refuse, agree, offer, learn, would prefer, be able to.
- **Bare infinitive (without to):** let sb do, make sb do, would rather do, had better do, modals (must/may/could) do.
  - **Passive istisna:** *make* edilgen olunca **to** alır → *"I was made **to** study."*
- **need + V-ing = passive anlamı:** *"The car needs cleaning." = "It needs to be cleaned."*
- **Anlam değiştirenler (\*):** stop, try, remember, forget.
  - *stop to smoke* (sigara içmek için durdu) ≠ *stop smoking* (sigarayı bıraktı)
  - *remember to lock* (kilitlemeyi unutma) ≠ *remember locking* (kilitlediğini hatırla)
  - *try to open* (açmaya çalış) ≠ *try opening* (bir de açmayı dene)
- **Preposition + V-ing:** *good at drawing.* **Phrasal verb + V-ing:** *look forward to seeing, give up smoking.*
- **Gerund özne olarak:** *"Drinking water is important."* **Sıfattan sonra inf:** *"It's important to drink water."*

**Pratik (IMG_9449 cevaplı):** to speak · going · have · to have · stay · working · to wear · live · to finish · going · not to tell · work · to study · to help · waiting · to park · getting up · not come · spending · to give · to buy · not to be · meeting · arriving · to revise.
